Basically, the goal is that you'll be divided into two teams: Fraud Squad and Fraudsters. Both of you will be given weapons and fight each other. It's a normal gun game.

Along with that, the Fraudsters have to commit frauds, so they will have to get the code generated by AI, fake heartbeats, and then inject their heartbeats and submit the project while the Fraud Squad is hunting them down. Once they complete all of it, they have to successfully go into the escape portal to claim their payout.

If they are successful, they win. If the Fraud Squad can stop them by inspecting their heartbeats and marking it as a fraud before they can escape, the Frauds Squad wins. 

It was intended to play live on stream.
